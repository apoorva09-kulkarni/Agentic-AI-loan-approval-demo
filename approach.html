Our Approach: The Two-Act Story

Act 1: The Lakehouse (Bringing Data Together)

Historically, bank data is trapped in silos. Credit officers manually pull structured data (Core Banking), unstructured data (PDF Salary Slips, KYC), and external data (CIBIL APIs).

Our Solution: We use a modern Data Lakehouse (like Google BigQuery) to ingest and unify all three data types into a single, real-time view of the customer. This eliminates the manual data-gathering phase entirely.

Act 2: The Agentic AI Layer (Reasoning & Action)

Having data is not enough; we need action. Instead of hardcoded rules, we deploy an AI Agent.

How it works: The Agent reads the applicant's profile from the Lakehouse. It decides which tools to use (e.g., calculating the FOIR), checks the policy constraints, and generates an explainable recommendation (Approve/Reject/Refer).

Human-in-the-Loop: The AI does the heavy lifting, but the final sanction button is always clicked by a human Credit Officer.
