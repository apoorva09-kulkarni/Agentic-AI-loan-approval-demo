About This Demo

This is a Point-of-View (POV) prototype designed for mid-sized banks looking to modernize their Retail Lending process. It was built to demonstrate the power of Agentic AI combined with a Lakehouse architecture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this using real customer data?

No. This demo uses entirely synthetic (generated) data to protect privacy and comply with data protection norms.

How does the AI make a decision?

The AI uses "Function Calling" to execute specific banking logic (like calculating the Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) and checks it against strict policy thresholds.
